03 · UK HOSPITALITY / VAT
lymon
UK HOSPITALITY
20% VAT here.
10% or less next door.
UK hospitality pays 20% VAT. France, Spain, Italy and Germany average 10% or less. Half our burden, sometimes a third. That gap is why Tom Kerridge's #VATsTheProblem campaign picked up over 100,000 signatures in three days. Three days. That's how strongly this sector feels it. Policy change matters, and I hope it comes. But I'm not sat around waiting on government to save the industry. Operators I talk to are looking for every way to run leaner and smarter with what they've already got. Lymon is one of those ways: a practical lever you can pull today, while the policy conversation plays out.

Lymon exists because you can't hire your way out of that. You have to organise your way out of it.

CIPD puts hospitality attrition at 52%, against a UK average of 34%. And 40% of leavers just move to the café down the road, chasing better hours or a manager with time for them. We built Lymon's rota tools because retention isn't a hiring problem, it's a Tuesday-shift problem: who covers when someone's sick, who trained the new starter, who noticed morale slipping. Fix the day-to-day, and the yearly number takes care of itself.
