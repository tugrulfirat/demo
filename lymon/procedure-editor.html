Front of House Closing Procedure — FOH Draftv1

This checklist runs based on its automation rules. Select a role tab to maintain the steps assigned to that team.

01Inform last customers of closing time politely.
02Clear all tables and ensure guests exit safely.
03Refrigerate and label items within use-by dates.
04Wipe all tables, chairs, and booths with sanitiser.
05Clean and restock toilets; replenish soap, towels, and sanitiser.
06Mop all FOH floors using approved chemicals.
07Turn off music, lighting, and heating or AC.
